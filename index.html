About Me

I am a UI/UX Designer focused on creating intuitive, user-friendly, and visually engaging digital experiences. I specialize in user interface (UI) design, user experience (UX) research, wireframing, prototyping, and responsive web design to develop digital products that balance usability and aesthetics. Using tools like Figma, I explore design solutions that address user needs while supporting business goals. I enjoy turning ideas into functional, accessible, and thoughtfully designed interfaces that make digital experiences easier and more enjoyable to use.
